Brands and manufacturers

  • Check how your products appear. Scan your own packs, or search by name, and see the score, the data source and the date the data was last updated.
  • Send us your label data, free. E-mail the barcode with clear photos or artwork of the front of pack, the ingredient list and the nutrition table. A reviewer checks the values against the label, and the record is shown as "Brand-submitted".
  • Reformulated or relabelled? Send the new label and we update the record. The score is recalculated by the same published rules as every other product.
  • What we can't do. We don't accept payment to change a score, hide a flag or appear in better choices.

Retailers and marketplaces

Meet shoppers at the moment they decide.

  • Store links from product pages. When a shopper finds a product or a better choice, a store button opens it in your app or on your site.
  • You keep the sale. SafePick shows no prices and runs no checkout. Price, stock and delivery stay with you.
  • Affiliate and referral programmes. We join retailer programmes where they exist and disclose them beside the buttons.
  • No effect on scores. Store links never change a score or the order of better choices.

Data providers and standards bodies

Authoritative product data, handled carefully.

  • Built on the barcode. SafePick identifies every product by its GTIN and validates the check digit across UPC-A, UPC-E, EAN-8, EAN-13 and GTIN-14.
  • Licensed data, clearly attributed. Today the catalogue combines open databases with reviewed submissions. We are adding licensed product data and welcome conversations with registries, data pools and brand-data services.
  • How we look after it. Provider credentials stay on our servers and never ship inside the app. Our database is hosted in Canada.
  • Credit where it's due. Each product page names its source and when it was last updated.
