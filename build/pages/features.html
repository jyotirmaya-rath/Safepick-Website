Features

Everything SafePick does when you point it at a pack.

A fast scanner, a clear verdict, better choices from the same shelf, and the small tools that make the next shop easier.

A scanner that answers on the spot.

Point the camera at the barcode. The result appears on the scanner itself, so you can keep the pack in your hand and keep moving.

  • Nothing to choose first. SafePick works out whether the pack is food, beauty or personal care from its barcode.
  • A quick result. The score, the verdict and the two or three reasons behind it, with "See full details" or "Scan another".
  • Scan the whole basket. Every pack you read stays in a strip at the bottom, so you can compare at the end.
  • Made for real aisles. A torch for dim shelves, tips when a code won't read, and a box to type the number in.
  • Clear when it can't help. "Not found" and "no connection" are told apart, and a missing product can be added in a minute.

Reads the UPC and EAN barcodes printed on retail packs (UPC-A, UPC-E, EAN-8 and EAN-13).

The product page: one clear answer, then the detail.

People scan fast in a store. The page leads with the verdict and folds everything else away until you ask for it.

  • A score with a word and a face. A number out of 100, a colour, a word and a face: Good, Okay or Avoid. Colour is never the only signal.
  • Nutrition highlights. Added sugar, saturated fat, fibre and more, each marked Low, Some or High, per 100 g or per serving.
  • Approved and Disapproved. The nutrients that help the product and the ones that hold it back.
  • Key takeaways. What's good, the main concern, and a sensible next step.
  • Declared allergens. Shown as the label declares them, never as "free from".

With Premium the page also opens the full ingredient list, how the score was worked out, the product's data source and checks, and questions about the product. See plans.

Better choices that are really better.

  • The same kind of product. Cookies for cookies, shampoo for shampoo. Never a salad instead of a snack.
  • Never a step down. An option is only called better when its score really is higher than what you scanned.
  • Respects the pack. A vegetarian product never gets a non-vegetarian suggestion.
  • Fits you. With your preferences saved, options that match them are marked "Fits your preferences".

Where store links are available, a button opens the product in the store's own app or site. SafePick shows no prices: you see the price in the store.

Look things up at home. Find them again later.

You don't need the pack in your hand. Search by name, browse a category, or open something you scanned last week.

  • Explore and search. Look a product up by name or brand, or browse food and beauty categories.
  • Your scan history. Everything you've scanned, grouped by today, yesterday and earlier, as a list or a grid.
  • Recommended for you. Better picks for the products you buy most, based on your scans and your preferences.
  • Stays on your phone. History is kept on your device. With Premium it can also follow you to a new phone.

Small things that make the next shop easier.

Works with a weak signal

Products you've opened are saved on your phone and shown with the date they were saved when the network drops.

App lock

Ask for Face ID, your fingerprint or your phone passcode each time SafePick opens.

Add a missing product

Photograph the pack and type the label. A reviewer checks it against your photos before anyone else sees it.

Report a problem

Spotted a wrong ingredient or an old label? Report it from the product page and a reviewer takes a look.

Your country, your language

Pick Canada, the USA or India on first launch. English and French in Canada, English and Spanish in the USA.

Reminders Premium

A nudge on weekend mornings to scan before you buy, and a reminder when it's time to buy something again.

Lists and favourites Premium

Save products to Favourites or your own lists and find them again in one tap.

History on all your devices Premium

Sign in on a new phone and your scan history is already there.

No ads, anywhere

Not on the free plan and not on Premium. Nothing on a product page is sponsored.
