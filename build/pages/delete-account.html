Delete in the app

  1. Open SafePick and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account & security.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. The app tells you when it's done.

Delete by e-mail

If you no longer have the app, e-mail {{email_privacy}} from the address you signed in with, using the subject "Delete my SafePick account". We may reply to confirm it's really you. We delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

Cancel Premium first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription, because Apple or Google bills it, not SafePick. Cancel it first, or you will keep being charged:

  • iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions.

A Supporter Pass ends on its own and needs no cancelling.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your e-mail address and account ID.
  • Your scan history kept on your account (Premium).
  • The copy of your health and dietary preferences, if you switched on "Keep on my account".
  • Meals you saved from photo estimates.
  • Your Premium status in our database.
  • Product submissions and reports that were never published, with their photos.
  • The records our subscription provider holds about your account, when no subscription is still running.
  • This account's scans on the phone you delete it from.

What is kept

  • Products you added that a reviewer already approved. They describe a product, not you, so they stay in the catalogue. They are no longer linked to your account.
  • Purchase records while a subscription is still running. They are needed to process renewals, refunds and cancellations, and are deleted once the subscription has ended.
  • Records held by Apple or Google about your purchases. Those belong to your App Store or Google Play account, which SafePick can't change.
  • Scans made while signed out stay on your phone until you clear them or delete the app.

Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove part of your data:

  • Scan history: use "Clear history" in the app. When you're signed in with Premium, this also clears the history on your account.
  • Health and dietary preferences on your account: switch off "Keep on my account". The copy on your account is deleted and your phone keeps its own.
  • Anything else: e-mail {{email_privacy}} and tell us what to remove.

If you never signed in

Guests have no account, so nothing about you is kept on our servers. Your scan history, saved products and preferences are only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
