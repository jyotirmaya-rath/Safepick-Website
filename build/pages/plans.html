Paying and cancelling

How do I pay?

Through the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android, with the payment method already on your phone. The price, including any taxes, is shown before you confirm. SafePick never sees or stores your card details.

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the month you already paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it first.

What is the Supporter Pass?

A one-time payment that gives you a full year of Premium. You choose the amount, and every amount gives the same year. It ends on its own: nothing renews and nothing is charged again.

What happens when I reach 1,000 scans on the free plan?

The app tells you how many free scans are left and the date they come back. The count starts again each month. Products you already scanned stay in your history.

Does Premium change a product's score?

No. Scores, verdicts and better choices are the same on every plan. Premium opens more detail about how a score was reached and adds personal tools.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules, because they process the payment. Ask through the App Store or Google Play.

Why does Premium need an account?

So it follows you to a new phone and can be restored. You sign in with Google or Apple. There is no password to remember. Scanning stays free without an account.
