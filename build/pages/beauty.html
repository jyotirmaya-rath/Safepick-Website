Beauty and personal care

Skincare, makeup and haircare, read from the ingredient list.

Scan a serum, a sunscreen or a shampoo the same way you scan food. SafePick reads the ingredient list on the pack and shows what's in it and what isn't.

What a beauty scan shows.

  • Listed or not listed. A quick checklist for parabens, sulfates, fragrance, mineral oil and drying alcohol. SafePick says "not listed", never "free from": it reports what the label declares, nothing more.
  • Key ingredients. The ones doing the work, such as glycerin, niacinamide or sodium hyaluronate, pulled out of a long list.
  • Skin match. How the formula suits oily, dry, sensitive and acne-prone skin, side by side.
  • A score and a verdict. The same Good, Okay or Avoid you see on food, worked out from the ingredient list only. No usable ingredient list means no score.
  • Well-rated options. Similar products with better-rated formulas, when there are some.

Have your own list of ingredients to avoid? Save it once and products that contain them are marked for you. Set your preferences.

A flag means "take a second look".

It isn't proof of harm. An ingredient list is a list, and there is a lot it can't tell anyone.

  • It doesn't show amounts. A trace and a main ingredient look the same on a label.
  • It doesn't show how a product is made or how and where on the body it is used.
  • It can't predict your skin. Patch-test new products, and stop using anything that bothers you.
  • It isn't a dermatologist. For a skin condition, ask a doctor or pharmacist what suits you.
