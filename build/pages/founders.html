About / Founders

The people behind SafePick.

SafePick Corporation was founded in British Columbia by two business owners: one who builds software, and one who runs a food business.

Jyotirmaya Rath

Co-founder, product and technology

Jyotirmaya designed and built SafePick, from the scanner and the scoring rules to the database behind them.

Jyotirmaya is the owner of JYO IT Consulting Ltd., a software company in Maple Ridge, British Columbia, and a Salesforce developer who builds mobile apps for iPhone and Android.

Phani Komi

Co-founder, partnerships and customers

Phani is the first point of contact for the brands, retailers and partners SafePick works with, and for the people who use it.

Phani is the owner of Pakka Local, an Indian food chain based in Surrey, British Columbia, and brings day-to-day experience of running a food business to the company.

Software and food, in one company.

A product scanner sits between two worlds. One is technical: barcodes, databases and rules that turn a label into a score. The other is the food business itself: what goes on a label, how products reach a shelf, and what customers ask before they buy.

SafePick was founded to hold both. Jyotirmaya builds the product. Phani works with the businesses and people around it.
