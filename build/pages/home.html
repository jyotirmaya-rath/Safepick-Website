One clear answer first. Details when you want them.

People scan fast in a store, so the page leads with what you need and folds the rest away.

  • A score with a word and a face. Every score shows a number, a colour, a word and a face, so colour is never the only signal.
  • Approved and Disapproved. The nutrients that help and the ones that hold a product back, per 100 g or per serving.
  • Better choices that are really better. Options come from the same kind of product and never score lower than what you scanned.

No ads. No tracking. Free to scan.

SafePick shows no advertising and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold.

  • Start as a guest. Scanning never needs an account. Your scans stay on your phone.
  • Scores can't be bought. No brand or store can pay to change a score, a ranking or the better-choices list.
  • How SafePick earns. Premium, and commissions from some store links at no extra cost to you.
