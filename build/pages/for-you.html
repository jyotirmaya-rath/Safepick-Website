The warning comes before the score.

A product can score well and still be wrong for you. So when a scan matches something you saved, SafePick says so at the top of the page.

  • Red for an allergy. A clear alert that names the allergen and tells you to avoid the product.
  • Amber for an intolerance or a goal. A notice, not an alarm: "Not the best choice for you".
  • Your numbers, pulled out. Watching sugar or sodium? The amount is shown with Low, Moderate or High beside it.
  • Better choices for you. Suggestions that match your preferences are marked "Fits your preferences".

What the allergy check can and can't do.

No app can promise a product is safe for your allergy. Here is exactly what SafePick does, so you know how far to trust it.

What it checks

The allergies and intolerances you saved, against the product's ingredient list, its allergen information and its name.

When it can't verify

If a product has no readable ingredient list, or only part of one, SafePick says the allergen can't be verified instead of staying silent.

A missing warning is not a green light

Product data can be incomplete or out of date. No warning never means a product is free of an allergen. Always check the pack, including "may contain".

Diet checks are preferences

Gluten-free and lactose-free are preference checks, not a coeliac-safe or allergy-safe guarantee.

Not medical advice. SafePick is an information tool. It can't know your health, your medicines or your diet, and it never replaces the package label or advice from your doctor, dietitian or pharmacist.

Health choices are personal. They stay with you.

What you save here is some of the most personal information in the app, so it gets the strictest treatment.

  • On your phone by default. Your choices are kept in your phone's secure storage and never leave it unless you say so.
  • "Keep on my account" is your call. Switch it on to copy your choices to your account so they appear on your other phones. Only you can see them.
  • Off means deleted. Switching it off deletes the copy on your account. Your phone keeps its own.
  • Never used for ads or analytics. Not shared with brands, stores or anyone else.
