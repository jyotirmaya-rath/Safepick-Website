Why we built it.

A food label holds a lot of information in very small print, and most of us read it in a few seconds with a cart in one hand. An ingredient list on a moisturizer is harder still.

SafePick turns that small print into one clear answer: a score, a word and the two or three reasons behind it. Then it shows what to pick instead.

We also wanted a scanner people could trust. That means no ads, scores that can't be bought, and plain statements about what a score doesn't know.

What we hold ourselves to.

The score is independent

No brand, store or partner can pay to change a score, a ranking or the better-choices list. Every product is scored by the same published rules.

No advertising

The app shows no ads on any plan and contains no advertising or tracking software. Nothing on a product page is sponsored.

Missing data is never guessed

If a label doesn't declare something, SafePick says so. With too little data there is no score at all. How scores work.

Your data stays yours

You can scan without an account. Your history and your health choices stay on your phone unless you choose otherwise. We don't sell personal information. Privacy policy.

Information, not medical advice

SafePick reads labels. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent anything, and it never replaces the pack or advice from a health professional.

Say what we don't know

Every product page shows where its data came from and when it was last updated, and the app says when something can't be verified.

How SafePick earns.

An app with no ads still has to pay its way. Here is exactly where the money comes from.

  • Premium and the Supporter Pass. Optional plans that add unlimited scans, full product details and personal tools. See plans.
  • Commissions from some store links. If you buy after tapping a store button, the store may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, SafePick earns from qualifying purchases.
  • Neither one touches the score. Scores, rankings and better choices are worked out before store links are added, and Premium shows the same scores as Free.

Canada first, then wider.

SafePick launches first in Canada, in English and French. The app also covers products sold in the United States and India, and you choose your country the first time you open it.
