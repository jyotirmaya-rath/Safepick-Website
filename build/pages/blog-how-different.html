There are several apps that scan a barcode and give a product a score. We think people should choose between them with their eyes open, so this article does not attack anyone. It lists six questions worth asking of any scanner app, including ours, and gives SafePick's answer to each.

1. Who pays for it?

If an app is free and full of advertising, the advertisers are the customers. SafePick shows no ads, on any plan. It earns from an optional Premium plan and from commissions on some store links, which cost you nothing extra. Both are disclosed in the app.

2. Can a brand change its score?

At SafePick, no. Every product is scored by the same published rules. Brands can send us their label data, and we check it against the label, but nobody can pay to change a score, hide a flag or appear in better choices. Store links are added after the score is worked out, never before.

3. What happens when data is missing?

This is where scores quietly go wrong. If a label doesn't declare fibre and an app treats that as zero, or ignores it, the score is misleading either way. SafePick never guesses. Nutrients a pack doesn't declare are shown as "Not on label". With partial data the score is capped so it can't read Good, and with too little data there is no score at all.

4. Does it know anything about me?

A score is the same for everyone, but people aren't. SafePick lets you save allergies, intolerances, a diet and health goals, and then checks every scan against them. A peanut allergy gets a red alert at the top of the page, above the score. This is free for everyone, and your choices stay on your phone unless you decide to keep a copy on your account.

5. Does it admit what it can't tell me?

No app can promise a product is safe for an allergy, and an ingredient list can't show how much of an ingredient is in a cream. SafePick says so, in the app, where it matters. It reports that an ingredient is "not listed", never that a product is "free from" it. It doesn't score baby food, supplements or alcohol, and it explains why. It describes the label and never claims to know what a food will do to your body.

6. What does it do with my data?

You can use SafePick without an account. Your scan history stays on your phone. There are no advertising or tracking tools in the app, and we don't sell personal information. Our database is hosted in Canada. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept and where.

Two more things

  • Food and beauty in one app. The same scanner reads a cereal box and a sunscreen, and works out which it is from the barcode.
  • A verdict you can read at a glance. A number, a colour, a word and a face: Good, Okay or Avoid. Colour is never the only signal.
