Good to know

When can I download SafePick?

SafePick is launching first in Canada, on iPhone and Android. The download buttons on this site switch on the day it reaches the App Store and Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start scanning as a guest. An account (Google or Apple) is only needed for Premium, for history on all your devices, and for adding a missing product.

Is SafePick free?

Yes. Everyone can scan 1,000 different products a month and get scores, Approved and Disapproved, allergen alerts, health and diet checks and better choices. Premium is optional. See plans.

Can SafePick tell me if a product is safe for my allergy?

No app can promise that. SafePick checks the allergies you saved against the ingredient list, the allergen information and the product name, and warns you when it finds a match. When a product has no readable ingredient list, it says the allergen can't be verified. A missing warning never means a product is free of an allergen: always check the label, including "may contain". What the allergy check can and can't do.

Where does the product information come from?

From Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, two open product databases, and from community and brand submissions that a reviewer checked against label photos. Each product page shows its source and when the data was last updated. More about the data.

I think a score is wrong. What can I do?

Scores come from the data on file, so first check the nutrition values and ingredients on the product page against the pack. If they differ, use "Report a problem". If the data is right and you disagree with the result, tell us at {{email_support}}.

Why doesn't baby food or salt have a score?

Some products aren't everyday foods to compare: baby food and infant formula, supplements, alcoholic drinks, and basics like salt, spices, plain tea, coffee and water. SafePick explains this on the page instead of showing a number that wouldn't mean much.

Does it work without a signal?

Products you've already opened are saved on your phone. With no signal, SafePick shows the saved copy and the date it was saved. A product you've never opened needs a connection the first time.

Which countries and languages does it support?

Canada (English and French), the United States (English and Spanish) and India (English). You choose your country the first time you open the app and can change it in Settings.

How do I cancel Premium?

In your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, not in SafePick. Step-by-step.
