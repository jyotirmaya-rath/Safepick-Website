Twenty directions for a new SafePick logo

Each concept has an app icon and the SafePick name set beside it. The files are vector (SVG), so they stay sharp at any size, and the letters are outlined, so no font needs installing. Pick one, or mix ideas, and it can go into the app, the website and the store listings.

The app's own score ring, from Avoid (red) through Okay (amber) to Good (green), around a check mark. It says "this app scores products" at a glance, and the dark background feels premium.
