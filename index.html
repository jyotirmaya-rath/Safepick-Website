Beauty and personal care

Skincare, makeup and haircare, read from the ingredient list.

Scan a serum, a sunscreen or a shampoo the same way you scan food. SafePick reads the INCI ingredient list and shows what's in it and what isn't.

  • Listed or not listed. A quick checklist for parabens, sulfates, fragrance, mineral oil and drying alcohol. SafePick says "not listed", never "free from".
  • Skin match. How the formula suits oily, dry, sensitive and acne-prone skin.
  • A flag means "take a second look". It isn't proof of harm: an ingredient list can't show amounts, how a product is made or how your skin will react. Patch-test new products.
  • Well-rated options. Similar products with better-rated formulas, when there are some.

How scores work

Honest about what a score can and can't tell you.

Food and drink scores compare what the label declares, per 100 g or 100 ml, with reference ranges and a few category adjustments.

  • Energy
  • Added sugar
  • Total sugar
  • Saturated fat
  • Trans fat
  • Sodium
  • Protein
  • Fibre
  • Ingredient list

Beauty scores use the ingredient list only. Wording describes the label, like "high in sodium per 100 g", never what a food will do to you.

Missing data is never guessed

Nutrients a pack doesn't declare are left out and shown as "Not on label". With too little data there's no number at all, and partial data caps the score at 59, so it can't read Good.

Some products aren't scored

Baby food and infant formula, supplements, alcoholic drinks, and basics like salt, spices, plain tea, coffee and water. The page says why instead of showing a number.

"Why this score?" on every product

The nutrient and ingredient flags, the positives, the method version, the data source and when the data was last updated.

Where the data comes from

Over a million products sold in Canada, the USA and India, from Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, plus community submissions checked against label photos.

Not medical advice. SafePick reads what's printed on a pack and turns it into a score and plain notes. It never replaces the label or advice from your doctor, dietitian, pharmacist or dermatologist, especially for allergies, diabetes, pregnancy, children and skin conditions.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start scanning as a guest. An account (Google or Apple) is only needed for Premium, for history on all your devices, and for adding a missing product.

Is SafePick free?

Yes. Everyone can scan 1,000 different products a month and get scores, Approved and Disapproved, allergen alerts, health and diet checks and better choices. Premium is optional and adds unlimited scans, full product details and personal tools.

Can SafePick tell me if a product is safe for my allergy?

No app can promise that. SafePick checks the allergies you saved against the ingredient list, the allergen information and the product name, and warns you when it finds a match. When a product has no readable ingredient list, it says the allergen can't be verified. A missing warning never means a product is free of an allergen: always check the label, including "may contain".

Where does the product information come from?

From Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, two open product databases, and from community submissions that a reviewer checked against label photos. Each product page shows its source and when the data was last updated.

What if my product isn't found?

You can add it: photograph the front, the ingredients and the nutrition table, and type what the label says. A reviewer checks it against your photos before anyone else sees it. Spotted a mistake on a product? Use Report a problem on its page.

Why doesn't baby food or salt have a score?

Some products aren't everyday foods to compare: baby food and infant formula, supplements, alcoholic drinks, and basics like salt, spices, plain tea, coffee and water. SafePick explains this on the page instead of showing a number that wouldn't mean much.

Does it work without a signal?

Products you've already opened are saved on your phone. With no signal, SafePick shows the saved copy and the date it was saved. A product you've never opened needs a connection the first time.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes, SafePick is made for both, from the same app.
