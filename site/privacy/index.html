Privacy policy

What the SafePick app and this website do with your information, in plain language.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version

  • You can scan without an account. Your scan history and your health and diet preferences stay on your phone unless you choose otherwise.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell personal information.
  • An account (Google or Apple sign-in) is only needed for Premium, for history on all your devices and for adding or reporting products.
  • Our database is hosted in Canada.
  • You can delete your account, and what we keep with it, at any time.

Who we are

SafePick is operated by SafePick Corporation, British Columbia, Canada ("SafePick", "we", "us"). This policy covers the SafePick mobile app for iPhone and Android and the website at safepick.app.

For privacy questions and requests, write to privacy@safepick.app.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers, and they are removed when you delete the app.

  • Scan history. The products you scan, shown in Recent scans and History. With Premium you can also choose to keep it on your account (see below).
  • Health and dietary preferences. Allergies, intolerances, health-related needs, diets, goals and ingredients to avoid. They are kept in your phone's secure storage and compared with products on your phone. They leave your phone only if you switch on "Keep on my account".
  • Saved copies of products. So the app works with a weak signal, it keeps a copy of recently viewed products (up to 200). Copies older than 30 days are marked as older, and are removed after 180 days. They hold product information only.
  • Lists and favourites. Products you save to lists.
  • Reminders. Buy-again and weekend reminders are scheduled by your phone as local notifications. The products and dates involved are stored only on your device. The app learns about a purchase only if you tell it. Notifications are never used for promotion, and you can switch them off in the app or in your phone settings.
  • Your country and language.
  • App lock. If you turn it on, your phone checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. SafePick is only told whether the check passed and never receives biometric data.

What we keep if you create an account

Scanning, scores, product details, better choices and store links never need an account. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we keep:

  • Your e-mail address and an account ID. Google may also share your name and profile photo with our sign-in service. SafePick does not use or show them. We do not ask Apple for your name.
  • Your Premium status, if you buy Premium or a Supporter Pass: whether it is active, when it ends, which product and which store.
  • Your scan history, with Premium. The barcode, name, brand, score, type of product, date, and a link to the pack photo when the data source has one, so the history appears on your other devices.
  • Your health and dietary preferences, only if you switch on "Keep on my account". Only you can read them. Switching the setting off deletes the copy on your account.
  • Products you add or report. The photos you take of the pack, the details you type (name, brand, ingredients, nutrition values, pack size), your reports, and the time. Brand submissions also include the company name and company e-mail address given.
  • Meals you save from a photo estimate, where that feature is available: the foods, portions and nutrition estimate you confirmed, and the time. The photo itself is not kept.

We use this information to provide the features you asked for, to check submissions before they are shown, and to prevent misuse, such as daily limits and blocking accounts that send false details. We do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your contributions. Your photos and a submission that is still waiting can be seen only by you and our reviewers. When a reviewer approves a product, its details and front-of-pack photo are shown to everyone, without your name. Your other photos stay private.

What is sent when you look up a product

To look a product up, the app sends the barcode number, or the words you type in search, to our own product database and, when needed, to the open databases Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts. Like any internet service, those services see your device's network address (IP address). Nothing else about you is sent with a lookup.

Camera and photos

  • Scanning barcodes. The camera reads the barcode on your device. These camera images are not saved, uploaded or shared.
  • Adding or reporting a product. Only if you choose to add or report a product, the app asks you to photograph the pack's label. Those photos are uploaded to our storage so a reviewer can check what you typed. Photos are never taken in the background.
  • Meal photo estimates (where available, and only when you are signed in). If you ask for a nutrition estimate from a photo of a meal, the photo, any note you add and your language are sent through our server in Canada to Anthropic, an AI company in the United States, which returns the estimate. Nothing that identifies you, such as your e-mail address or account ID, is sent with it, and SafePick does not keep the photo. To apply fair-use limits we record that your account made a request and when; those records are removed after two days. Keep faces and documents out of the photo.

Premium and payments

  • Payments are handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Subscription status. To know whether your Premium is active we use RevenueCat, Inc. as our subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives your SafePick account ID and your purchase records from the store (product, dates, renewal status, store country). We have switched off RevenueCat's optional collection of device identifiers. If you never sign in, the app never connects to RevenueCat.
  • Cancelling. You manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. Deleting your SafePick account does not cancel it.

Store links and affiliate links

Store buttons open shops such as Amazon and Walmart and, in India, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket, Flipkart, JioMart and Nykaa. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Some of these links are affiliate links: if you buy after tapping one, the shop may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. The shop or its affiliate network may learn that you arrived from SafePick and the product name used for the search. We do not receive your name, address, payment details or what you bought. Scores, better choices and search results are never influenced by affiliate links.

Where your information is stored

Our database and file storage are hosted in Canada. Some of the providers above process information in the United States or other countries. When information is processed outside Canada, it is subject to the laws of that country.

We protect information in transit with encryption (HTTPS), restrict each account to its own records with access rules in the database, and keep service keys on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe and will tell you if a breach puts you at real risk.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you clear it or delete the app.
  • On your account: until you delete it or delete your account. Requests made by e-mail are completed within 30 days.
  • Contributions: submissions and reports that were never published are deleted with your account. A product that a reviewer already approved stays in the catalogue, because it describes a product, not you; it is no longer linked to your account.
  • Photo estimate request records: two days.
  • Subscription records: if a subscription is still running when you delete your account, its store records are kept until it ends, so renewals, refunds and cancellations can be processed.
  • E-mails you send us: as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your choices and rights

  • Use SafePick without an account. Scanning never needs one.
  • See, correct or delete your information. Most of it is in the app. For anything else, write to privacy@safepick.app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings, Account & security, Delete my account) or by e-mail. See Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by switching off "Keep on my account", reminders or camera access.

We answer requests from everyone the same way, wherever you live. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law. If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the privacy commissioner of your province.

Children

SafePick is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

safepick.app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we set no cookies of our own. The company that hosts the site keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to deliver and protect it. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write.

Health information

SafePick does not provide medical advice. Scores and notes are general information based on product label data, which can be incomplete or out of date, and they are not a guarantee that a product is suitable for you or free of any allergen. For allergies, diabetes, pregnancy, children, medical diets or skin conditions, always check the physical package and ask a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also announce it in the app or on this website. If the app ever starts measuring usage, we will update this policy and the store privacy answers first, and ask for your consent in the app.
